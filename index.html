About me

Zonghui Wen

Hi, there! I am Zonghui Wen, a Ph.D. student at Xiamen University.

My previous research has focused on computer vision and small object detection, including lightweight detection models for driving and UAV scenarios.

I am currently interested in embodied AI and intelligent agents. I am always happy to connect and collaborate on related research—feel free to reach out through Google Scholar or GitHub.
