About me

Zonghui Wen

Hi, I am Zonghui Wen (温宗辉), a Ph.D. student at Xiamen University, supervised by Prof. Liaoni Wu and Prof. Yancheng You.

Previously, I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Technology from Capital Normal University under the supervision of Lecturer Yongxiang Zhang.

My research interests include embodied AI, intelligent agents, and computer vision. I am always happy to connect and collaborate on related research—feel free to reach out.
